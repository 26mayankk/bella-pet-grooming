Which pets and services can I request?

Bella Pet Grooming offers grooming for dogs and cats. Current website packages include Signature Bath, Complete Grooming and custom grooming requests. Share your pet’s needs so the team can advise on a suitable service.

Which areas do you serve?

The website currently lists doorstep appointments in Bhopal. Share your locality in the appointment form so the team can check whether the requested visit can be accommodated.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form sends an appointment request only. Your date and time are not confirmed until Bella Pet Grooming contacts you and confirms availability.

Can the final price change?

The listed package prices are a starting point. Breed, coat condition, temperament and requested add-ons may affect the final quote. Please confirm the price with the team before grooming begins.

What should I mention before the grooming visit?

Share your pet’s coat needs and any relevant handling or comfort concerns. If your pet has severe matting, skin concerns or becomes distressed during handling, mention this in advance so the team can discuss an appropriate plan.
